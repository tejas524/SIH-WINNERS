The problem

Airfare inflation has been flying blind.

Official airfare measurement in India still relies on manual, 30-day airport price collection — too slow, too thin, and too opaque for monetary policy that needs to react in days, not months.

01

30-day manual collection

Archaic airport surveys capture a sparse snapshot while fares move daily across airlines, OTAs and booking windows — the signal is stale before it is published.

02

No real-time index

The transport CPI sub-group lags the market by weeks, leaving the RBI Monetary Policy Committee without a timely airfare signal when it matters most.

03

Untraceable numbers

Policy debates cite airfare figures with no provenance — no source quotes, no methodology, no audit trail behind the headline claim.

Methodology

UN/ILO-aligned index mathematics.

Statutory continuity by default, superlative quality by standard. AirIntel India reports the Laspeyres index for MoSPI continuity alongside the Fisher ideal as the quality benchmark.

1

Jevons elementary aggregate

Geometric mean across quotes per route × booking-window cell — eliminates arithmetic-mean bias at the elementary level.

2

Laspeyres master index

Fixed-base, DGCA passenger-volume-weighted aggregate (base Jan 2024 = 100) — the statutory MoSPI baseline.

3

Superlative Fisher & Törnqvist

√(Iₗ·Iₚ) and log-share alternatives capture substitution effects the fixed-basket Laspeyres overstates.

4

CPI transmission

Airfare moves map into transport-CPI bps (8.59% weight) and headline bps (0.33% effective) with DGCA route-level attribution.

5

MAD outlier rejection

Modified Z-score filter (|M| > 3.0) drops corrupt quotes before aggregation; deduplication keeps the best-sourced listing per flight.

Architecture

A pipeline that reads like a route network.

Collection, econometrics, forecasting and delivery in one FastAPI platform — deployable via Docker or Vercel, with a full REST catalog for integration.

Layer 1 · Collect
Ethical scraper engine
Token-bucket rate-limited collection from airline-direct portals, OTA aggregators and government sources — robots.txt compliant.
Layer 2 · Clean
Validation & provenance
Deduplication, MAD outlier rejection and SHA-256 hashing into the provenance vault.
Layer 3 · Index
Econometric engine
Jevons · Laspeyres · Paasche · Fisher · Törnqvist · Walsh index construction with substitution-bias analysis.
Layer 4 · Forecast
Multi-model ensemble
ETS · autoregressive · GBDT nowcast models with walk-forward selection and 95% confidence cones.
Layer 5 · Decide
Policy simulation & AI analyst
Scenario engine with CPI-bps projections, alerting, daily dossiers and a data-grounded AI policy analyst.
Layer 6 · Deliver
FastAPI REST + live feeds
60+ versioned endpoints, OpenAPI catalog, WebSocket/SSE live index ticks, and a zero-CDN single-file command center UI.
